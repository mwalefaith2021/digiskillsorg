ABOUT DIGI SKILLS

A youth-led movement to bridge Africa's digital divide .

We are young Africans building the digital community we wished we grew up with and had, which is accessible, practical, and rooted in our own context.

WHO WE ARE

Young Africans for young Africans.

Digi Skills is a youth led initiative with people who believe digital skills should not be a privilege. We design programs, mentorship and community spaces that meet learners where they are.

WHY WE EXIST

Only 9% of African youth aged 15–24 have basic computer skills. Without intervention, the digital divide will continue to lock millions out of the modern economy. We exist to change that.

Our Vision

A generation of digitally skilled African youth who create opportunities, drive innovation, and contribute to sustainable development.

Our Mission

To provide accessible digital skills education, mentorship and learning opportunities that prepare young people for the evolving digital world.

CORE VALUES

What we stand for.

Innovation

We build, prototype and reimagine what learning can look like.

Collaboration

We grow faster together, across cohorts, partners and borders.

Inclusion

Programs designed for learners regardless of background or geography.

Lifelong Learning

Skills evolve and so do we. Curiosity is a core competency.

Empowerment

We hand the tools, the voice, and the stage to young people.

HOW WE WORK

Four levers of impact.

Training

Practical bootcamps and short courses that build real-world skills.

Mentorship

One-on-one and group guidance from professionals in tech.

Community

Peer networks, study groups and a space to keep building.

Partnerships

Working with schools, orgs and sponsors to expand reach.

WHO WE SERVE

Built for every young learner with ambition.

  • Secondary school students exploring tech for the first time.
  • University students building skills beyond the curriculum.
  • Recent graduates preparing for the digital job market.
  • Young innovators turning ideas into real projects.
  • Underserved communities historically left out of digital education.
FUTURE VISION

The Digi Skills Innovation Hub.

Over the next decade, we are building a permanent home for African digital learning — a hub for AI, web development and youth-led innovation, with physical and virtual spaces across the continent.
